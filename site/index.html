Who we are

Owner-led building, start to finish

Turant Builders is a custom home builder rooted in New Palestine, Indiana. We partner with families and individuals across the Indianapolis area to design and construct homes that fit their land, their routines, and their taste — not a catalog.

Because the people whose name is on the company are the same people on your project, decisions stay close, communication stays direct, and the standard never slips between the first sketch and the final walkthrough.
